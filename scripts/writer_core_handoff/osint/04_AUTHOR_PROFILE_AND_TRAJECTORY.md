# OSINT 04. Author Profile & Trajectory

AuthorProfile is a distribution, not a fingerprint point.

Stores centroid/variance per feature family, sample size, time range, domain, genre, language and known collaborators/tooling.

`AuthorTrajectory` models drift across time. Sudden changepoints create `StylisticRegime` candidates rather than automatic claims about hidden contributors.

Span-level segmentation may identify distinct regimes inside a document, but contributor identity remains a calibrated hypothesis.
