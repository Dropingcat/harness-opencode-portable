# Linguistic Benchmark and Golden Fixtures

The linguistic subsystem is not trusted because it has many diagrams. It earns authority through adversarial fixtures.

## Benchmark families
1. agreement/government;
2. clause segmentation;
3. coreference and event reference;
4. negation/quantifier scope;
5. modality/causal force;
6. scope and qualifiers;
7. comparison and numeric semantics;
8. attribution/source voice;
9. thematic progression/cohesion;
10. discourse relations/connectives;
11. presupposition/implicit warrant;
12. academic sentence realization;
13. controlled compression/expansion;
14. author-style preference vs journal/template confounders.

## Required metrics
Per error class: precision, recall, false-block rate, escalation rate, repair success, Tier-2 utilization, human-overturn rate.

## Model-tier evaluation
A weak model MUST be benchmarked with and without Linguistic Digest/tooling. The subsystem is useful only if structured tooling measurably improves correctness/cost over raw prompting.

## Golden policy
Fixtures store expected semantic differences and acceptable variants, not one sacred wording.
